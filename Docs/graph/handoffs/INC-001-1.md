## 7. Decisions and assumptions

- Correlation key is `(rule_id, device)`; all policy windows are caller supplied, with no hidden default.
- Evidence keeps the 20 most recent relevant samples, 64 relevant missing-signal names, and 20 live snapshot references per incident/work intent.
- New incidents atomically advance `Open -> Investigating`; both creation and transition remain separate recorded/tested state-machine events.
- A firing during `Verifying` is the diagram's recurrence edge and emits a new work intent; a duplicate firing on an already active investigation does not.
- Resolved recurrence uses the dedicated reopen window rather than the correlation window.
